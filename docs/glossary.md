# Glossary

**BBCode**
:   The formatting code used by forums, such as `[b]bold[/b]`. Tick
    **BBCode output** on the Scorinate step to get results ready to paste.

**Bracket**
:   The draw for a knockout cup: who plays whom in the first round, and
    how the winners meet after that.

**Bye**
:   An empty place in a knockout bracket. The team drawn against a bye
    goes through to the next round without playing.

**Conference**
:   A group of teams within a league split into conferences or divisions.
    In an inter-conference round robin, each group is one conference: teams
    play their own conference and every other conference too.

**Event**
:   One competition you generate results for, such as a group, a cup or a
    final. A file can hold many events.

**Formula**
:   A set of rules for deciding results in a sport. Association football
    has several, such as LISA, NSFS and SQIS. Pick the one your
    competition uses.

**Group**
:   The participants who compete against each other in an event. In a
    league, a group plays a round robin. In a race, a group is one heat or
    one final.

**Leg**
:   One meeting between two teams. Two legs means they play home and away.

**Matchday**
:   One round of fixtures, generated together. A knockout cup's rounds
    have names instead, such as "Quarter-finals".

**Participant**
:   One entry in an event: a team in team sports, or an athlete in
    individual sports.

**Round robin**
:   A format where everyone in a group plays everyone else in that group.

**Scorinate**
:   To generate results. "Scorinating an event" means letting xkoranate
    play it.

**Seed**
:   A ranked entry in a knockout draw, placed so that the strongest teams
    don't meet early.

**Signups**
:   The list of participants in an event, with their team and skill.

**Single elimination**
:   A knockout cup: lose once and you're out.

**Skill**
:   How strong a participant is, also called their rank. Higher is
    better. How skill numbers are compared depends on the formula; SQIS,
    for example, compares them as a ratio.

**Team (code)**
:   The short code for a participant's nation, such as `AUR`.

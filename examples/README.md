# Example scorination files

Ready-made events for trying things out. Open one with **File → Open** (or point
your event directory at this folder in Settings).

## example-season.xml — The Racing Scorinator

A complete twelve-round racing season, set up and ready to scorinate:

- **30 drivers** in 15 two-car teams, with reliability, aggression and technique
  ratings, plus 5 tyre manufacturers and 5 engine manufacturers. All four kinds
  of entrant appear on their own tab at the **Signups** step.
- **A twelve-week calendar** at the **Calendar** step, covering every qualifying
  format — traditional, one shot, two tier and elimination — a wet round or two,
  and two `Testing` weeks that run practice only and score nothing.
- **An RP list** with a score for each of the 30 nations on the entry list, so
  the RP bonus weighting has something to work with.

Twenty-two matchdays in all: qualifying and a race for each of the ten race
weeks, plus a practice session for each of the two testing weeks. Go to
**Scorinate** and work down the matchday list; the drivers' and constructors'
championships build up as you go, and regenerating an earlier matchday rewinds
them.

The drivers, teams and circuits are the placeholder cast from the original
Racing Scorinator spreadsheet, so nothing here is anyone's real team.
